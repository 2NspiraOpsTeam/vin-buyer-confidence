## Core Positioning

### One-sentence positioning

`VIN Buyer Confidence is a buyer-decision tool that helps you decide whether a used car is worth the deposit, the PPI, or the trip before you spend more money.`

### Messaging hierarchy

#### Promise

- Decide whether this car is worth more money and effort.

#### Proof

- Cross-check the VIN, the listing story, the ask, and the available evidence.
- Separate what is confirmed from what is partial, unresolved, or not yet available.
- Turn weak spots into next-step guidance and copy-ready seller questions.

#### Use cases

- Before sending a deposit.
- Before paying for a PPI.
- Before traveling to see the car.
- Before convincing yourself to chase a listing that does not hold up.

#### Next action

- Enter the car details.
- Review what is confirmed and what still needs verification.
- Decide whether to pursue, negotiate, verify further, or walk away.

## What This Is / Is Not

### This is

- A buyer-confidence review.
- A decision-support workflow.
- A way to pressure-test the seller story before larger spend.

### This is not

- A generic VIN decoder.
- A Carfax clone.
- A replacement for a licensed inspection.
- A legal guarantee or title warranty.

## Initial Buyer Segments

### Remote buyer

- Pain point: worries about traveling for a car that falls apart under closer scrutiny.
- Value prop: sort serious listings from time-wasters before flights, hotels, shipping decisions, or time off.

### Private-party buyer

- Pain point: seller claims are informal, records are inconsistent, and the buyer has to do the filtering alone.
- Value prop: verify the story, identify the soft spots, and know what to ask without sounding confrontational.

### Enthusiast / spec-sensitive buyer

- Pain point: trim, options, originality, and maintenance details matter more than a generic “clean VIN” answer.
- Value prop: cross-check spec claims and missing evidence before overpaying for a story.

### PPI / deposit decision buyer

- Pain point: they do not want to waste inspection money or lock into the wrong car too early.
- Value prop: use the review as a gate before the next spend.

## Language To Avoid

- Avoid `clean report` unless the exact scope is defined.
- Avoid `verified car` when only partial evidence exists.
- Avoid `buy with confidence` as a blanket promise.
- Avoid language that implies title, inspection, legal, or mechanical guarantees.
- Avoid positioning that sounds like a generic VIN lookup product.

## Seller Question Templates

### Missing service records

- `Can you share any recent service invoices or maintenance records, especially from the last 20,000 miles?`
- Why this matters: service gaps increase ownership-risk uncertainty.

### Unclear options or trim

- `Can you send close photos or a short video that clearly shows the claimed options, trim details, and interior features?`
- Why this matters: higher-value spec claims should be supported before the buyer pays a premium.

### Accident or title uncertainty

- `Has the car ever had an accident, insurance claim, salvage event, or title issue that I should know about? If so, can you share the details and any repair documentation?`
- Why this matters: unresolved history risk changes whether the car is worth a deposit or inspection.

### Photo gaps

- `Can you send a slow walkaround and interior video, plus close photos of the areas that are not visible in the listing?`
- Why this matters: missing visual evidence leaves too much room for condition surprises.

### Pricing mismatch

- `Can you help me understand the asking price versus the condition, records, and recent maintenance?`
- Why this matters: if the evidence is thin, the price needs stronger justification.

### Recall status

- `Do you know of any open recalls or recall work that has already been completed?`
- Why this matters: unresolved recall work may affect timing, safety, or next-step cost.

### Mileage concern

- `Can you share dated service or inspection records that help confirm the current mileage story?`
- Why this matters: mileage confidence supports both valuation and condition expectations.

## Trust / FAQ / Objection Handling

### FAQ: Is this a Carfax replacement?

- `No. This is a buyer decision tool. History inputs matter, but the goal is to help you decide whether this specific car is worth more money and effort.`

### FAQ: Does this replace a PPI?

- `No. It helps you decide whether a PPI is justified, what the inspector should verify, or when it is smarter to walk away first.`

### FAQ: What if data is missing?

- `Missing data is treated as uncertainty, not as a clean bill of health.`

### Status copy

- `Confirmed`: supported by strong source evidence.
- `Partial`: some evidence is present, but important support is still missing.
- `Needs verification`: plausible, but not strong enough to rely on yet.
- `Not connected`: that evidence layer is not available yet, so silence should not be treated as proof.

### Honest disclaimer

- `This workflow helps buyers make better pre-commitment decisions, but it does not replace mechanical inspection, legal review, title verification, or licensed third-party reports.`
