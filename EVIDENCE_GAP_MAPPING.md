# Evidence-Gap Mapping for Seller Questions

## Overview

Seller questions should be generated based on missing/partial evidence in the review. Each question should be tied to a specific risk or evidence gap.

## Evidence Gaps and Corresponding Questions

### 1. VIN Identity Gaps
**Gap**: VIN decode is incomplete or unclear
**Questions**:
- Can you confirm the VIN is correct and matches the vehicle?
- Is this the original VIN or has it been replaced?

### 2. Options/Features Gaps
**Gap**: Claimed features need stronger evidence
**Questions**:
- Can you send more detailed photos confirming the key options/features?
- Is the sunroof/moonroof original or after-market?
- Are the wheels original equipment or after-market?

### 3. Service History Gaps
**Gap**: Service history appears thin for the price/mileage
**Questions**:
- What major maintenance has been done recently?
- Can you share service records for the past 2 years?
- Has the vehicle been regularly serviced at a dealership?

### 4. Title/Accident Uncertainty
**Gap**: Title status or accident history needs verification
**Questions**:
- Has the vehicle ever been in a major accident?
- Is the title clean or does it have any brandings (salvage, rebuilt, flood)?
- Has the vehicle ever been declared a total loss?

### 5. Photo Evidence Gaps
**Gap**: Photos don't fully match listing description
**Questions**:
- Can you share more photos of the interior?
- Can you show the condition of the tires and brakes?
- Can you share a video walkaround of the vehicle?

### 6. Price Mismatch Gaps
**Gap**: Asking price seems high for the condition/mileage
**Questions**:
- How did you arrive at this asking price?
- Have you had the vehicle appraised?
- Are there any recent repairs or upgrades that justify the price?

### 7. Recall Status Gaps
**Gap**: Recall status needs verification
**Questions**:
- Have all open recalls been addressed?
- Can you show proof of recall repairs?

## Implementation Notes

- Questions should be dynamically generated based on the evidence gaps
- Each question should include a "why this matters" explanation
- Questions should be useful before deposit, PPI, or travel
