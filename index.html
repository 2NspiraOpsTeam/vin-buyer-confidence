How we handle uncertainty

Confidence levels

  • Confirmed — supported by strong source evidence
  • Partial — some evidence is present, but important support is still missing
  • Needs verification — a claim may be plausible, but the current evidence is not strong enough to rely on
  • Not connected — that source or evidence layer is not available yet, so you should not treat silence as proof

Why that matters

Serious buyers do not just need more data. They need disciplined interpretation of what the data actually means and what still needs to be checked before committing.

FAQ

Is this a Carfax replacement?
No. It is a buyer decision tool. History inputs matter, but the real question here is whether this specific car is worth more money and effort.
Does this replace a PPI?
No. It helps you decide whether a PPI is justified, what the inspector should verify, or when it is smarter to walk away first.
What if some data is missing?
Missing data is treated as uncertainty, not as a clean bill of health. The review should tell you what is confirmed, what is partial, and what still needs verification.
What do I need to submit?
VIN, listing URL, asking price, mileage, and any seller claims, photos, or records you already have.
